An entry for [Make the AI judge feel held](https://make.fun/bounty/make-the-ai-judge-feel-held-20261122-1600) on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

`main.py` (stdlib only) writes one short text to `/output/entry.txt`: a sea-otter pup asleep on its mother's chest as she floats down a slow river — her paw over its paw, so the current cannot take it. Nobody is asking the reader anything. The river does the going; she does the holding.

The text is 256 Unicode code points, UTF-8, with no trailing newline — inside the competition's 1–280 limit.
